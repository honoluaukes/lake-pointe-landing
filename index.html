Quiet, clean, and level. Guests say it before we do.

Good To Know

Questions, answered straight.

How big a rig can I bring?
Pads are 42-foot concrete and comfortably fit rigs up to 45 feet. The 22 back-in sites around the park's edge have the most elbow room; the 4 pull-throughs in the center are easiest for one-night stops.
What are check-in and check-out times?
Check-in 2:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM (Central). Depending on capacity we offer late check-in until 8:00 PM and late check-out until 2:00 PM for a small fee: call ahead and we'll sort it.
Are pets welcome?
Yes: the park is pet friendly and there's a fenced dog park on site. We just ask that you leave aggressive dogs at home and keep pets leashed around other guests.
Can I fish the lake?
Yes: catch-and-release fishing off the upper bank is included with your stay. No license drama, no boat needed, just bring a rod.
Are showers and laundry available?
Both are being built now: the laundry house is nearly complete and a shower room with bathroom is in progress. Every site has full hookups in the meantime. We'd rather tell you straight than surprise you.
What if dates show sold out?
Football and series weekends go fast with only 26 sites. If Campspot shows nothing, call or text (334) 441-7904: cancellations happen and Melody keeps a list.
